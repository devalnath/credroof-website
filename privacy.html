Privacy note

Plain language, because you are being asked to send your name and phone number to a stranger.

What we collect
The details you type into the enquiry form: your name, WhatsApp number, district, area, property type, budget, timeline, purpose and anything you add in the notes. If your enquiry comes from an advertisement, we also record which advertisement sent you.
Why we collect it
To contact you about your enquiry, to check what funding is possible for your profile, and to look for a home in the location you asked for.
What we do not do
We do not sell your details, and we do not share them for other companies' marketing. We do not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, salary slips or bank statements before you agree to a lender pre-check.
Who may see it
The CredRoof team handling your enquiry. At the pre-check stage, and only after you agree, the relevant details are shared with the lender or the legal reviewer working on your case. We will name them when that happens.
How long we keep it
Enquiry details are kept while your case is active and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can answer questions about it. If your enquiry goes no further, you can ask us to delete it.
How to have it removed
Enquiries are not being accepted yet, so there is nothing to remove at this point. Before the site starts accepting enquiries, a working removal route will be published here. Until then, if someone holds your details, ask that person directly to delete them.
