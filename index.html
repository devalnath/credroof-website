              Do I really get up to 20% below market value?
              

                

                  On selected properties, yes. Each one is priced against comparable homes nearby, and we show you the
                  comparison before you commit. It is not every property, which is why we say selected properties,
                  subject to valuation.
                

              

            

            

              Do I really get up to 100% funding?
              

                

                  Up to 100% funding is the ceiling we work towards with our partner banks and finance companies, for
                  eligible applicants. We check what is possible for your profile at no cost and put the terms in
                  writing before you commit. Approval, rate and tenure are the funding partner's decision.
                

              

            

            

              What do I pay, and when?
              

                

                  Nothing to start: the first conversation and the eligibility check are free, and there is no charge
                  to apply. The price is agreed per property. Stamp duty, registration and other statutory costs are
                  paid at registration and stay your cash, along with any arrears, repairs and moving costs.
                

              

            

            

              Which areas do you cover?
              

                

                  First branch in Thrissur, enquiries from all 14 districts. Tell us the place you want and we search
                  within about 10 km of it. Availability depends on your area and budget.
